# OATM Presentation — Outline (10 slides)

Your hook goes directly before slide 1 — there's no separate opening/pivot
slide anymore, since neither carried real content once you have the hook.

1. **Title** — full-bleed hero photo, reveal after your hook.
2. **The Problem** — 3-frame filmstrip (before → occluded → after).
3. **Research Question & Hypothesis**
4. **Evidence 1: Baseline** — method (1 line) + both charts + stat ribbon,
   merged onto one slide.
5. **Evidence 2: Naive Memory** — best/worst images + a one-line mention of
   the other 3 samples + stat ribbon, merged onto one slide.
6. **Evidence 3: SORT Motion Prediction** — both charts + stat ribbon + a
   compact "THE CATCH" callout box with the 4 methodological caveats
   condensed into one paragraph, merged onto one slide.
7. **Diagnosis + The Gap**
8. **OATM Design**
9. **How OATM Compares** (table)
10. **Roadmap + Close**

## What changed from the 15-slide version

- Cut the opening slide (was blank) and the pivot slide (was just re-echoing
  your hook's last line) — 2 slides, zero content lost.
- Merged each evidence act's "results" slide into its "setup" slide — 3
  slides cut, content compressed but not removed (Evidence 2 keeps the
  per-sample numbers as a text line instead of a 5-image gallery row).
- Every number is still traceable to the same source files as before:
  `results/final_report.md`, `results/last_seen_memory/summary.csv`,
  `results/sort_paper_experiment/summary.csv`.
